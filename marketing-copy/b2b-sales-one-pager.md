## B2B Executive Sales One-Pager

### Solution Overview
AQA Events offers an enterprise prepaid activity credit platform designed for modern HR and office managers across Algeria. Elevate team well-being, foster company culture, and boost employee satisfaction through flexible sports, wellness, and team-building experiences.

With AQA Events B2B, your organization purchases a centralized credit pool and manages employee allocations through a secure, self-service Corporate Portal.
